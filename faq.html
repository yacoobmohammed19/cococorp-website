General
What does CocoCorp do?

CocoCorp is a South African technology company that builds professional websites and delivers AI coaching for small businesses. We serve clients across South Africa and internationally. Our services include website design from R3,500 once-off, local SEO, Google Ads management, AI coaching, and custom AI chatbot deployment.

Does CocoCorp work with clients outside South Africa?

Yes. We are based in South Africa and deliver all work remotely. We work in the SAST timezone (UTC+2), which overlaps well with European and UK business hours. Payments can be made in ZAR, USD, GBP, or EUR.

How do I contact CocoCorp?

The fastest way to reach us is via WhatsApp at +27 84 731 4960. You can also use the contact form on our website or chat with our AI assistant for instant answers. We respond to all enquiries within 2 hours during business hours (Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm SAST).

Websites & SEO
How much does a website cost?

Once-off builds start from R3,500 for a basic one-page site. Monthly subscriptions start from R500/month. Full business websites start from R6,500 once-off. See our full pricing page with currency toggle for exact conversions.

Can I try before I pay?

Yes. We build a custom landing page demo for your business at no cost. If you love it, take it live. If you are not happy, you owe us nothing. This is completely risk-free with no obligation.

How long does it take to build a website?

A starter landing page is typically ready within 48 to 72 hours. Full business websites take 5 to 10 business days. We use AI-powered build processes to deliver faster than traditional agencies.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive and mobile-optimised. Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices, so mobile performance is a core requirement in every build.

What is included in the monthly subscription?

Monthly subscriptions include website hosting, SSL certificate, mobile optimisation, and content updates (1 to 3 per month depending on your plan). Plans range from R500/month (Starter) to R750/month (Pro) which includes priority support and analytics setup.

Do you help with Google Ads and SEO?

Yes. SEO setup starts from R400 with ongoing monitoring from R200/month. Google Ads management is available as ad-hoc campaigns (R800-R1,000) or monthly management at a percentage of your ad spend. Both are designed to help you get found by local and international customers.

AI Services
How can AI help my small business?

AI automates repetitive tasks like writing marketing content, responding to customer enquiries, processing documents, and strategic planning. Free tools like ChatGPT and Google Gemini can save you 5 to 10 hours per week. Our AI services page explains each use case in detail.

Do I need technical skills to use AI?

No. The tools we teach (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Canva AI, Claude AI) are designed for non-technical users. You interact with them using plain English. Our workshops are specifically built for business owners with no tech background.

How much do AI services cost?

Team AI Training sessions start at R2,500 for up to 20 participants. The 1-on-1 Executive Programme is R5,000 for 5 sessions plus one month of WhatsApp support. An ongoing AI Retainer is priced case by case. See the AI pricing section for full details.

Can you build an AI chatbot for my website?

Yes. We build custom AI chatbots trained on your business information. The chatbot sits on your website and handles customer enquiries 24/7, captures leads, and routes prospects to you. The service is R1,500/month and includes monthly knowledge base updates and support.
