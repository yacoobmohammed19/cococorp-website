About CocoCorp

Our "Why"

CocoCorp is a Johannesburg-based technology company that builds professional websites and delivers AI solutions for small businesses. Based in Midrand, Gauteng, the company serves clients across South Africa and internationally. Services include website design from R1,500 once-off (approximately $80 USD), local SEO management, Google Ads, AI chatbot deployment, and business process automation training.

We see entrepreneurs doing amazing things every day. But sometimes you don't need to work harder, you need to work smarter.

As founders with over 10 years of experience implementing data and technology solutions for large companies, we wanted to bring the latest tech to the small businesses that form the backbone of the global economy.

Our mission is to help you unlock more value, gain more customers, and build a more profitable business through practical, meaningful technology.
